Privacy and disclosures

Last updated November 25, 2026

Rossen Reports (rossenreports.com) shares Jeff Rossen’s deals, videos and scam alerts. You can use the whole site without an account, and we don’t ask for your name, email or any other personal details.

Affiliate links

Many deal and store links on this site are affiliate links. When you buy something through one, Rossen Reports may earn a commission from the store, at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate, Rossen Reports earns from qualifying purchases. We also earn from purchases made through our Walmart creator links.

Prices and availability come from the stores and can change at any time, especially during sales. The price you see at the store when you check out is the one that applies.

What we collect

  • Visit statistics. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and see which pages are popular. It doesn’t use cookies, and it doesn’t identify you or follow you to other sites.
  • Saved deals. When you tap the heart on a deal, it’s saved in your own browser only. We never receive that list. Clearing your browser’s site data removes it.
  • Server logs. Like every website, our hosting provider (Vercel) briefly keeps technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run the site and protect it from attacks.

We don’t sell or share personal information, and we don’t run advertising trackers.

Other companies’ services

  • YouTube. Videos play in YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player, which doesn’t set YouTube cookies until you press play. Once a video plays, Google’s privacy policy (opens in a new tab) applies.
  • Product photos load from Amazon and Walmart, and fonts and icons load from Google Fonts and cdnjs. Your browser connects to those services to show them.
  • Stores and other sites. Links take you to sites we don’t control, such as Amazon, Walmart, YouTube and government agencies. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Scam alerts

Scam alerts come straight from public feeds published by the FTC, CFPB, U.S. Department of Justice and state attorneys general. They’re for general information. If you think you’ve been scammed, report it to the FTC (opens in a new tab).

Children

This site is meant for adults and isn’t directed at children under 13.
