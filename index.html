Who is Jeff Rossen

On Your Side

"I'll save you money by revealing the best deals, and exposing the worst scams."

— Jeff Rossen, Rossen Reports

2025

Went fully independent — launched his own YouTube channel and consumer media brand with 2.4 million followers across platforms.

2019

Joined Hearst Television — Rossen Reports syndicated nationally across NBC, ABC, and CBS affiliate stations.

2017

Published Rossen to the Rescue — the ultimate guide to avoiding scams, everyday dangers, and major catastrophes.

2012

Launched Rossen Reports — named National Investigative Correspondent; segments aired on Today, Nightly News, and Dateline NBC.

2008

Joined NBC News — became a fixture on the Today Show and NBC Nightly News with Lester Holt.
